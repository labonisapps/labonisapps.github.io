Electron microscopy tools

Four Android apps for work at the microscope. Each one does a single job well enough that you can finish it at the instrument, instead of taking the data back to a desk.

Everything runs on your device. Nothing you open is sent to a server.

Apps

  • IndexonisNot on Google Play yet Reads a photograph of a selected-area electron diffraction pattern, reads the scale bar, detects the spots and gives you d-spacings, the zone axis and hkl — read more
  • CrystonisNot on Google Play yet Opens a crystal structure file and draws the atomic arrangement in 3D, viewed down any zone axis — read more
  • SpectronisNot on Google Play yet Pick the elements in your specimen and see the EDX lines and EELS edges together, which ones overlap, and what each technique can actually tell you — read more
  • PacbedonisNot on Google Play yet Calculates CBED, PACBED and STEM images of perfect crystals by the multislice method, and estimates specimen thickness from a PACBED you photographed — read more

Languages

The apps are bilingual: the interface is English by default and switches to Korean on a Korean device.
